Overview
Product details

The Invesco S&P 500® Equal Weight ETF (Fund) is an index fund that seeks to track the results of the S&P 500® Equal Weight Index (Index).The Index equally weights the stocks in the S&P 500® Index. This approach results in an exposure that tilts toward smaller companies in the S&P 500 Index.

Relative to market cap indexes like the S&P 500, this reduces the concentration risk.

RSP and the Index are rebalanced quarterly.

Why invest in RSP?

Over twenty years ago, the Invesco S&P 500 Equal Weight ETF (ticker: RSP) helped reinvent how clients access the S&P 500. If you're invested in funds that track the S&P 500 Index, your portfolio may be too concentrated and missing out on potentially higher returns. RSP can help address both concerns with the added potential benefits of cost-effectiveness and tax efficiency. 

Reduce concentration risk

The fund’s equal weight approach has the potential to mitigate the concentration risk of the market-cap-weighted S&P 500 Index.

Complement large-cap exposure

RSP’s equal weight methodology provides greater exposure to smaller companies in the S&P 500 Index, leveraging the size and value factors.

Access at an attractive cost with tax efficiency

RSP has among the lowest expense ratio versus its peers and has not paid capital gains distributions since inception.1
