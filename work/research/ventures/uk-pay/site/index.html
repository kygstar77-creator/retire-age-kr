How it's calculated

Take-home pay is your salary minus Income Tax, employee National Insurance (category A) and any student loan repayments, worked out for a full tax year from 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027 and divided by 12 for the monthly figure. It assumes the standard tax code (1257L) with no other income, benefits in kind or pension deductions.
