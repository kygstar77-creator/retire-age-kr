UK Take Home Pay2026/27

About

UK Take Home Pay is an independent calculator. It shows your monthly pay after Income Tax, National Insurance and student loan, and how much of your next £1,000 you keep. It has no adverts and no sign-up.

It is not connected to HMRC or GOV.UK. Rates come from GOV.UK pages, which are linked under each calculator with the date we last checked them.

What it covers

  • Employees in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, 2026/27 tax year (6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027)
  • Standard tax code 1257L, employee National Insurance category A
  • Student loan Plans 1, 2, 4 and 5, and the Postgraduate loan

Scotland, self-employment, other tax codes, dividends and savings income are not covered yet.

Corrections

If a figure looks wrong, email retireage.kr@gmail.com with the salary you entered. We can't promise a reply, but we check every report against GOV.UK.
